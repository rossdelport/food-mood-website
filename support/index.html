Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Food Mood is local-first. The food and mood entries you create are stored on your own device, not on our servers. You can learn more in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete all of your data at any time by clearing the app's storage on your device or uninstalling the app. Because your entries live on your device, removing them is fully in your control.

Will my entries sync between devices?

Because Food Mood keeps your data on your device by default, entries are not automatically synced across devices. If you have feedback on this, we'd love to hear from you at the email above.

I found a bug or have a feature request — what should I do?

Please email us at rossdelport1998@gmail.com with as much detail as you can. Bug reports and ideas genuinely shape what we build next.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases made through the Apple App Store are handled by Apple. You can request a refund directly through Apple's refund process.
