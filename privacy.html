Privacy Policy

Food Mood · Last updated 10 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Food Mood (“Food Mood”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the Food Mood application and website (the “App”). We have designed Food Mood to keep your personal information private by default. Please read this policy carefully.

1. Summary

  • Food Mood is local-first: the food and mood entries you create are stored on your own device, not on our servers.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising networks or sell data to data brokers.
  • You can delete all of your data at any time by clearing the App's data on your device.

2. Information We Collect

Information you create. When you log meals, moods, notes, or related entries, that content is stored locally on your device using your browser or device storage. By default this information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Technical information. Like most software, the App may process basic technical information needed to run, such as device type, operating system version, and app version. Where the App is delivered through the Apple App Store, Apple may collect certain information in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information you choose to send us. If you contact us for support, we receive the information you include in your message (such as your email address and the contents of your message).

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App's core functionality — letting you record and review how food makes you feel.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

4. Legal Bases (EEA/UK Users)

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process information on the basis of: performing our agreement with you (to provide the App), our legitimate interests (to operate and improve the App), and your consent (where required). You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. How Information Is Shared

Because your entries are stored locally, we generally have no personal entries to share. We may share limited information only:

  • With service providers who help us operate the App, who are bound to protect it and use it only on our instructions;
  • With Apple, to the extent required to distribute and support the App via the App Store;
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

Any third party with whom information is shared is required to provide the same or equal protection of your data as described in this policy.

6. Data Retention

Entries you create remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App. Support messages are retained only as long as needed to assist you and to keep reasonable records.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and deletion. You control your entries directly on your device. To delete everything, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.
  • GDPR rights. EEA/UK users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to data portability.
  • CCPA rights. California residents may have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA.

To exercise any right, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keeping your device secure also helps protect your entries.

10. International Users

The App may be used worldwide. If you access the App from outside the country where any of our service providers operate, your information may be processed in other countries with different data protection laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be highlighted within the App or on this page.
